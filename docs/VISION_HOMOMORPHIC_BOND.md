## Executive Summary

The homomorphic bond is the foundational structural property of our network architecture: **judgment without possession**—a rhyme with homomorphic encryption's compute-without-seeing.

A network built from these organs can match, judge, govern, and pay for patterns it never carries. That is the primitive for networks that protect without surveilling.

## 2. The Two Trust Laws

1. **Judgment Without Possession**: Protection that does not require seeing. The matcher holds addresses of the forbidden, never the forbidden; the companion holds the shape of your voice, never the recording; the guide holds the gradient, never a file on you.
2. **Loyalty With a Published Duty Layer**: Loyal to the person up to the immutable core, and the core is public. She will tell you, before you ever speak, exactly what she cannot keep between you—and that promise is hash-chained like everything else.

Escalation under this design is not betrayal of the bond; it is the bond's deepest clause: **your life outranks your secrets, and you knew that from the start**.
